About Ashlyn

Ashlyn Hulsey holds a PhD in Sport Psychology from Florida State University. She's a Certified Mental Performance Consultant (CMPC) and a Registered Mental Health Counselor Intern (RMHCI).

She has extensive experience with Division I collegiate athletes, teams, and performers: one-on-one, in team workshops, and side by side with coaches, athletic trainers, strength and conditioning staff, and academic support.

Areas of focus

Confidence Emotional regulation Performance anxiety Identity development Leadership Communication Team dynamics Pressure performance

Approach

Ashlyn pairs evidence-based mental performance strategies with therapeutic principles. The aim: resilience, self-awareness, and performance that lasts. Chasing a peak, working through a transition, or dealing with something personal, the work starts with a strong relationship and a plan built for that one person.

Dual credentialing

Because Ashlyn holds credentials in both mental performance and mental health counseling, she can integrate the two when clinically appropriate. That flexibility matters when performance work touches deeper themes: identity, transition, grief, or the anxiety and mood shifts that can come with high-performance life.
